The Heritage of Ferrari

Founded by Enzo Ferrari in 1939 out of Alfa Romeo's race division under the name Auto Avio Costruzioni, Ferrari built its very first car in 1940. However, the company's iconic badge—the legendary "Prancing Horse" (Cavallino Rampante)—was born from a tribute to World War I flying ace Francesco Baracca.

Today, Ferrari is globally synonymous with elite luxury, high-performance sports cars, and an unmatched legacy in Formula 1 racing. Every vehicle that leaves the factory in Maranello, Italy, blends heart-stopping speed with pure artistic craftsmanship.
